Affiliate and advertising disclosure

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Current retailer links

Our current Amazon and Walmart buttons open ordinary retailer search pages. They are not affiliate-tracking links, and CartSprout does not currently earn commissions from them.

Future affiliate relationships

If we join an affiliate programme, some links may include tracking. CartSprout may then earn a commission when a visitor makes a qualifying purchase through an approved link. Any such relationship will be clearly identified near the relevant links and explained on this page.

Advertising

CartSprout does not currently display paid advertisements. If an advertising service is approved and activated, advertisements will be identified as advertising and this page will be updated.

Retailer information

Retailers control product descriptions, prices, stock, checkout, shipping, customer service and returns. Please review the retailer's current information before making a purchase.
