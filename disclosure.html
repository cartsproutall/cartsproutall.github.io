Affiliate and advertising disclosure

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Current status

CartSprout currently links to ordinary Amazon and Walmart search pages. These are not affiliate-tracking links. CartSprout does not currently earn commissions from these links and does not currently display paid advertising.

Future affiliate links

If CartSprout joins an affiliate programme, some links may be special tracking links. If a reader makes a qualifying purchase through an approved link, CartSprout may receive a commission at no additional cost to the reader. Any such relationship will be clearly disclosed before or near the relevant links, and this page will be updated.

Advertising

If an advertising service is approved and activated, advertisements will be identified as advertising. Advertising availability and personalization may depend on the provider, visitor location and applicable consent requirements.

Retailer responsibility

Retailers control their own product information, prices, availability, checkout, shipping, customer service and returns. Always review the retailer's current terms before purchasing.
