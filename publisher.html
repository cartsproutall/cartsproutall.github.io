Publisher information

CartSprout is an independent, web-based shopping-discovery publication. It provides category browsing and general buying guides for everyday consumer products.

Publication scope

Our current coverage includes grocery and pantry basics, household items, kitchen tools, cleaning supplies, home organization, home-office accessories, travel accessories and pet supplies.

Editorial standards

Guides explain comparison criteria and are not represented as hands-on product tests. We aim to correct factual errors and distinguish editorial information from any future paid placement or affiliate relationship. Read our editorial policy.

Current monetization status

CartSprout currently uses ordinary retailer search links. No affiliate tracking programme or advertising network is connected through this website at the time of this update. No commissions or advertising earnings are being recorded or paid through CartSprout.

Payments and accounts

This website does not accept customer payments, process orders or hold a visitor's payment details. Purchases, payment processing, delivery and returns take place with the retailer. Any future publisher payment arrangements will be handled by the relevant approved programme and its supported payout methods.

Contact

A dedicated public contact address will be added after an official CartSprout email account is established. We do not publish an unverified address.
